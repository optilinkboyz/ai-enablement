# AI Assistant — Best Practice Guidelines

**Guidelines for responsible and effective AI use in companies**

---

## 1. When to Use the AI Assistant

### ✅ Good use cases
- Quickly understanding a long document before a meeting
- Getting a plain-language explanation of a technical report
- Extracting action items, deadlines, or key figures
- Preparing a summary to share with colleagues
- Answering general knowledge questions about AT&S or the industry

### ⚠️ Use with care
- Documents containing personal employee data (check with HR)
- Draft contracts or legal documents (always verify with Legal)
- Financial figures — always cross-check against the source

### ❌ Do not use for
- Making final decisions without human review
- Uploading **Confidential** or **Restricted** documents without approval
- Replacing professional judgement on safety, quality, or compliance matters

---

## 2. Data Handling Rules

| Rule | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Do not upload confidential documents without approval | Text is sent to an external AI service |
| Check classification before uploading | AT&S data classification policy applies |
| No personal data unless necessary | GDPR compliance |
| Documents are not stored — but be mindful | Good practice for any AI tool |
| Close browser or clear results when done | Prevents unintended access on shared machines |

---

## 3. Getting Accurate Results

**The AI can:** summarise, explain, extract information from documents, answer general questions
**The AI cannot:** know things not in the document, verify accuracy, access other AT&S systems

**Always verify:** numbers and figures, deadlines, names and responsibilities, anything you will act on

---

## 4. Writing Good Questions

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Tell me about this document" | "What are the three main risks identified in this report?" |
| "Dates?" | "List all deadlines and what needs to be delivered by each" |
| "Is this good?" | "What are the strengths and weaknesses of the proposed approach?" |

**Formula:** Specific action + specific topic + desired format = good question

---

## 5. Sharing AI-Generated Content

- Always label AI-generated summaries: *"AI-generated summary — please verify against the original"*
- Do not present AI output as your own analysis without adding judgement
- Review before sending — you are responsible for what you share

---

## 6. For Team Leads: Introducing the Tool

1. Share this guide and the User Guide with your team
2. Run the 10-minute demo (see Demo Script)
3. Start with low-risk documents — meeting minutes, internal reports
4. Collect feedback and escalate suggestions to Corporate IT
