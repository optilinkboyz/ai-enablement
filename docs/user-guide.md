# AT&S AI Assistant — User Guide

**For all AT&S employees — no technical knowledge required**

---

## What is this tool?

The AT&S AI Assistant helps you understand documents and get answers to your questions quickly. Think of it as a smart colleague who has read your document and can explain it to you in plain language.

You can:
- **Upload a document** (PDF, Word, or text file) and ask questions about it
- **Get a summary** of any document — short, medium, or detailed
- **Ask general questions** — even without uploading a document

---

## Getting Started (3 steps)

### Step 1 — Open the tool
Open your web browser and go to the AT&S AI Assistant link provided by Corporate IT.

You will see two panels:
- **Left side** — where you upload documents and ask questions
- **Right side** — where the AI responds

### Step 2 — Upload a document (optional)
1. Click the **"Click or drag a file here"** box on the left
2. Choose a PDF, Word document, or text file from your computer
3. Wait a few seconds — you will see **"✅ [filename] — X characters extracted"**

### Step 3 — Ask or Summarise
**To ask a question:**
1. Type your question in the text box
2. Click **"Ask →"** or press **Enter**
3. Read the answer on the right side

**To summarise a document:**
1. Click the **"📝 Summarise"** tab
2. Choose **Short**, **Medium**, or **Detailed**
3. Click **"Summarise →"**

## Example Questions to Try

| If you have... | Try asking... |
|---|---|
| A quality report | *"What are the main non-conformances mentioned?"* |
| Meeting minutes | *"List all action items and who is responsible"* |
| A technical spec | *"Explain this in simple terms for a non-engineer"* |
| A policy document | *"What are the key rules I need to follow?"* |
| A supplier contract | *"What are the payment terms and delivery deadlines?"* |
| No document | *"What does AT&S do?"* |

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific** — *"What is the deadline for Q3 delivery?"* works better than *"Tell me about dates"*
- **Ask one thing at a time** — you can always ask a follow-up
- **Use the summary first** — for long documents, summarise first, then ask specific questions
- **Copy the answer** — click **"📋 Copy"** to paste into an email
- **Start fresh** — click **"🗑️ Clear Results"** to reset

## FAQ

**Is my document stored anywhere?**
No. Documents are processed in memory only and never saved.

**What file types can I upload?**
PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx), plain text (.txt). Max 10MB.

**The AI says "I cannot find that in the document"?**
The information is not in the uploaded document. Try rephrasing or check the right document is uploaded.

**Can I use this for confidential documents?**
Follow AT&S data handling policies. Do not upload confidential documents without checking with your manager or Corporate IT.

**Can I ask in German?**
Yes — the AI understands German. Responses are clearest in English.
