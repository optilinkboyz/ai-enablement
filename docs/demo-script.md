# AT&S AI Assistant — Demo Script

**10-minute team demonstration guide for team leads and Corporate IT**

---

## Before You Start
- [ ] Open the AT&S AI Assistant in your browser
- [ ] Prepare a non-confidential sample document (internal report or meeting minutes)
- [ ] Share your screen if remote

---

## Opening (1 min)
> "Today I want to show you a new tool from Corporate IT — the AT&S AI Assistant. It helps you understand documents faster and answer questions about them. It takes 30 seconds to learn."

---

## Part 1 — General Question (2 min)
1. Type: **"What does AT&S do?"** → click **Ask →**
2. While waiting: *"Notice I didn't upload anything — the AI answers general questions too."*
3. When response appears: *"Clear, plain-language answer. Notice the 📋 Copy button — paste it straight into an email."*

---

## Part 2 — Upload + Summarise (3 min)
1. Upload your sample document → wait for **✅ extracted**
2. Click **📝 Summarise** → choose **Short** → **Summarise →**
3. *"In seconds we have a summary. Now let's see Detailed."*
4. Choose **Detailed** → **Summarise →**
5. *"See the difference — structured summary with key points and action items. Great for meeting prep."*

---

## Part 3 — Specific Question (2 min)
1. Click **❓ Ask a Question**
2. Type: *"What are the main action items in this document?"* → **Ask →**
3. *"Instead of scrolling 20 pages, I ask a direct question and get a direct answer."*

---

## Closing (1 min)
> "Three things to remember: **Upload** any PDF or Word doc, **Ask** questions or **Summarise**, **Copy** the answer wherever you need it. The User Guide and Best Practices doc have more details. Try it with your next report."

---

## Common Questions
**"Is my document safe?"** — Processed in memory, not stored. Follow AT&S data policies for confidential docs.
**"Can it read German?"** — Yes, but responses are clearest in English.
**"What if it's wrong?"** — Treat it as a smart first draft. Always verify important details.

---

- [ ] Ask people to try one real document this week
- [ ] Collect feedback and forward suggestions to Corporate IT
